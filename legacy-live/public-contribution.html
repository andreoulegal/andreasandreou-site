Public contribution

Policy becomes real when someone is waiting for an answer.

Local government is where good intentions meet procedures, budgets, suppliers and the everyday expectations of citizens.

Galatsi · Athens

Close to the consequences.

As a Municipal Councillor and former Deputy Mayor responsible for the Press Office, I learned that communication is inseparable from the institution behind it. A request that looks simple from the outside may cross departments, systems and constraints before it reaches a citizen.

That experience sharpened my interest in procurement, digital government and the practical design of public institutions — not as abstract policy, but as systems people encounter. During this period, I helped oversee the transfer of the Galatsi Olympic Hall, securing €1.2m in annual municipal income through commercial leases and enabling savings and land acquisitions for future green urban space.

What public service taught me
01

Implementation is policy.

The quality of a decision is tested by whether an institution can deliver it.

02

Trust is operational.

Clear communication and visible accountability are part of how institutions work.

03

Local insight scales.

What happens in a municipality can reveal lessons for wider public systems.

From local experience to better systems

Public value is built one practical decision at a time.
